Generation method: built-in image_gen (imagegen skill), not the local API/CLI.

Identity presentation prompt:
Use case: logo-brand.
Create one finished, original logo identity presentation for "Agent Remote", a real iPhone + macOS utility that lets a person send a task from their phone to Qwen Code or Codex on a remote computer, then follow execution. Positioning: quiet capable developer tool, connection plus delegated action, reliable, approachable, precise.

Art direction: exceptionally simple custom geometric symbol, strong memorable silhouette, suitable for a 20px macOS monochrome menu bar icon and a square iOS app icon. The symbol should abstract a task being handed from one endpoint to another: two bold rounded open-corner/terminal forms offset diagonally, whose shared negative space creates a short decisive forward/up-right arrow. Merge these into ONE compact balanced glyph, not a diagram or an assembly of stock icons. Optical spacing, substantial uniform weight, generous open counters. Feel like a crafted professional tool identity. Do not draw literal phones, computers, robot heads, clouds, wifi waves, sparkles, infinity loops, globe icons or a network graph. No generic AI knot. No fine decorative lines.

Use the deep forest teal of the existing app (#20594C), warm off-white (#F5F6F2), charcoal (#172622), with pale mint used only as a very restrained supporting surface. The glyph itself must work in one solid ink color. Flat vector-like edges, no gradients, no 3D chrome, no glow, no bevel, no photographic props.

Presentation: a beautifully spaced wide landscape identity sheet, approximately 3:2, warm off-white background, generous margins and editorial alignment. Main large hero area: the custom mark in forest green beside the exact wordmark "Agent Remote", rendered in exceptionally clean contemporary humanist/grotesk sans serif, medium weight, natural kerning, title case. This main lockup dominates the upper half. A thin quiet rule separates the lower applications row: (1) the identical glyph in ivory centered on one deep-green softly rounded square app tile, generous internal padding; (2) same glyph in solid charcoal on ivory; (3) same glyph in solid ivory on a forest-green rectangular field. These are the SAME exact logo, not alternative designs. Small minimal labels only: "APP ICON", "MONO", "REVERSED". Small heading upper-left "AGENT REMOTE / IDENTITY 01". No slogan or invented body copy. No device mockups. No extra ornamental marks. The result should feel like a concise real identity proposal ready to judge, not a busy brand collage.
